AquaTrack+ sequence: Transect 045-048
=====================================

This folder is a sequence: 4 videos that AquaTrack+ plays back to back as one timeline (2264 frames, 01:15).

How to open it
  - Open the project (.aqp) in AquaTrack+, then choose "Transect 045-048" on the Dashboard or the Videos page.
  - Or, without the project: Dashboard > Open media..., and pick the
    sequence.json in this folder.
  A media player opens the videos one at a time only; this folder is
  what joins them.

The videos are referenced, not copied: keep them where the paths above
point (paths are relative to this folder). To change the order, add or
remove clips, use Edit sequence... in AquaTrack+ rather than editing
sequence.json by hand, so annotations move with their clips.
